B.TECH CSE — CURRICULUM OVERVIEW
=======================================================================

Duration: 4 Years (8 Semesters)
Total Credits: ~160 credits

Semester 1 (First Year):
- Mathematics I (Calculus & Linear Algebra)
- Physics / Chemistry (based on group)
- Introduction to Programming (Python / C)
- Engineering Drawing
- Communication Skills (English)
- Environmental Science
- Lab: Programming Lab

Semester 2 (First Year):
- Mathematics II (Discrete Mathematics)
- Physics / Chemistry (alternates from Sem 1)
- Data Structures
- Digital Electronics
- Engineering Workshop / Mechanics
- Lab: Data Structures Lab, Digital Electronics Lab

Semester 3 (Second Year):
- Mathematics III (Probability & Statistics)
- Object-Oriented Programming (Java / C++)
- Computer Organization and Architecture
- Operating Systems
- Database Management Systems (DBMS)
- Labs: OOP Lab, OS Lab, DBMS Lab

Semester 4 (Second Year):
- Design and Analysis of Algorithms
- Computer Networks
- Software Engineering
- Theory of Computation
- Elective I
- Labs: Networking Lab, Project Lab

Semester 5 (Third Year):
- Compiler Design
- Artificial Intelligence
- Machine Learning
- Elective II
- Elective III
- Mini Project

Semester 6 (Third Year):
- Information Security / Cyber Security
- Cloud Computing / Distributed Systems
- Deep Learning
- Elective IV
- Elective V
- Internship / Research Project

Semester 7 (Fourth Year):
- Major Project (Part I)
- Elective VI
- Elective VII
- Seminar

Semester 8 (Fourth Year):
- Major Project (Part II / Final)
- Technical Communication

Popular Electives (CSE):
- Natural Language Processing (NLP)
- Computer Vision
- IoT (Internet of Things)
- Blockchain Technology
- Robotics
- Data Mining & Big Data Analytics
- Game Development

=======================================================================
Ph.D. PROGRAM
=======================================================================

Areas: CSE and ECE (same research areas as M.Tech)

Admission Process:
- Conducted TWICE a year: Autumn Semester (July) and Spring Semester (January)
- Step 1: Online Application at iiitp.ac.in
- Step 2: Shortlisting based on GATE / NET / JRF score + academic record
- Step 3: Written Test
- Step 4: Technical Interview with faculty panel
- Step 5: Final merit list and seat allotment

Eligibility:
- M.Tech/M.E./M.S. in relevant field (CSE/ECE/IT/EEE) with 60%+ marks
- Candidates with B.Tech + GATE (exceptional profile) may be considered for direct Ph.D.

Fellowship Schemes:
1. Institute Fellowship: ₹31,000/month (JRF), ₹35,000/month (SRF) — 5 years
2. Visvesvaraya Ph.D. Scheme (MeitY): Additional benefits for IT/electronics research
3. Sponsored: Fee paid by organization; salary as per employment contract
4. Part-time Ph.D.: Available for working professionals

Duration: Minimum 3 years (typically 4–5 years)

FACULTY
=======================================================================

IIIT Pune has a growing faculty with Ph.D.s from IITs, IISc, and reputed international universities. Many faculty members:
- Are actively involved in funded research projects (DST, MeitY, SERB, DRDO, ISRO)
- Have publications in IEEE, ACM, Springer, Elsevier journals
- Have industry collaborations with companies like TCS, Infosys, NVIDIA, Qualcomm

Faculty-Student Ratio: Approximately 1:15 (improving as institute grows)

=======================================================================
ACADEMIC POLICIES
=======================================================================

Attendance:
- Minimum 75% attendance mandatory in each course to appear for exams
- Students with less than 75% may be debarred from semester exams

Grading System:
- 10-point CGPA scale
- Minimum CGPA of 5.0 required to pass / continue
- Minimum CGPA of 6.0 required for placement eligibility in most companies

Examinations:
- Mid-Semester Exam: ~30% weightage
- End-Semester Exam: ~50% weightage
- Assignments/Labs/Quizzes: ~20% weightage

Backlogs:
- Students can re-appear in failed courses in subsequent semesters
- Maximum attempts allowed: Usually 3 attempts per course
