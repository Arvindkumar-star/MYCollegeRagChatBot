=======================================================================
PLACEMENT PROCESS AT IIIT PUNE
=======================================================================

Timeline:
- Phase 1: August–December (Pre-placement talks + Internship season)
- Phase 2: November–March (Final placement drives)

Process for Students:
1. Register on the Training & Placement (T&P) portal
2. Submit resume through the T&P cell
3. Attend pre-placement talks (PPTs) by companies
4. Appear in online aptitude tests / coding assessments
5. Technical interviews (2–3 rounds typically)
6. HR interview
7. Offer letter issuance

Eligibility for Placement:
- Minimum CGPA requirement varies by company (typically 6.0–7.5 CGPA)
- No active backlogs (company-specific)
- Must be in final year (B.Tech 4th year)

=======================================================================
INTERNSHIPS
=======================================================================

- Duration: Typically 2 months (May–June, after 3rd year B.Tech)
- Many companies offer Pre-Placement Offers (PPO) post internship
- Students can also pursue internships independently (startups, research labs)
- Internship stipends range from ₹15,000–₹80,000 per month

=======================================================================
HIGHER EDUCATION / FURTHER STUDIES
=======================================================================

Students also pursue:
- M.Tech / M.S. at IITs and NITs via GATE
- MBA at IIMs
- MS/Ph.D. abroad (USA, Europe, Canada) at top universities
- UPSC / Government Services

Typically 10–15% of graduating batch opts for higher studies over placement.
