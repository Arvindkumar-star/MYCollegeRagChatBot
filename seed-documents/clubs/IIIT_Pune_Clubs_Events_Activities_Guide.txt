INDIAN INSTITUTE OF INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY PUNE (IIIT Pune)
Student Clubs, Events & Campus Activities Guide
Source: Official IIIT Pune website, student community pages, campus reports
Last Updated: 2024

=======================================================================
OVERVIEW
=======================================================================

IIIT Pune has a vibrant student community with 15+ active clubs covering technical, cultural, sports, and social domains. Despite being a relatively young institute (est. 2016), the student community has built a rich campus culture.

=======================================================================
TECHNICAL CLUBS
=======================================================================

1. COMPETITIVE PROGRAMMING CLUB (CPC)
   - Focus: Competitive coding, algorithm solving
   - Platforms: Codeforces, LeetCode, CodeChef, AtCoder
   - Activities: Weekly contests, coaching sessions, team formation for ICPC
   - Achievement: IIIT Pune students have achieved Global Rank < 500 on Codeforces
   - Contact: cpc@iiitp.ac.in

2. ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE & MACHINE LEARNING CLUB (AIMl)
   - Focus: ML/DL projects, Kaggle competitions, research paper reading
   - Activities: Workshop series, Kaggle team competitions, guest talks
   - Projects: NLP tools, computer vision applications, recommendation systems

3. ROBOTICS CLUB
   - Focus: Hardware + software robotics, embedded systems
   - Activities: Line-follower, maze-solver robots, national robotics competitions
   - Collaboration: Robotics competitions at IIT Bombay, NIT events

4. CYBERSECURITY CLUB (Infosec / CTF Team)
   - Focus: Capture The Flag (CTF) competitions, ethical hacking
   - Platforms: HackTheBox, PicoCTF, TryHackMe
   - Achievement: Participated in national and international CTF events

5. WEB DEVELOPMENT CLUB
   - Focus: Full-stack development, open source contribution
   - Activities: Hackathons, building tools for campus use, GitHub projects

6. ELECTRONICS CLUB
   - Focus: Circuit design, PCB design, Arduino/Raspberry Pi projects
   - Activities: Hardware projects, workshops for beginners

7. OPEN SOURCE CLUB
   - Focus: Contributing to open-source projects (GitHub), GSoC preparation
   - Achievement: Students have received Google Summer of Code (GSoC) selections

=======================================================================
CULTURAL CLUBS
=======================================================================

8. DRAMATICS CLUB (NAATAK)
   - Focus: Street plays, stage performances, one-act plays
   - Events: Performances during Iconclave, intra-college drama competitions

9. MUSIC CLUB
   - Focus: Instrumental and vocal music
   - Instruments: Guitar, tabla, keyboard, drums available
   - Events: Open mic nights, cultural evenings, Iconclave performances

10. DANCE CLUB
    - Focus: Western dance (freestyle, hip-hop), classical Indian dance forms
    - Events: Freshers' welcome, Iconclave, inter-college competitions

11. FINE ARTS CLUB
    - Focus: Painting, sketching, digital art, poster design
    - Events: Art exhibitions, campus beautification projects, poster competitions

12. PHOTOGRAPHY CLUB
    - Focus: Campus photography, event documentation, photo walks
    - Events: Photo contests, workshops on DSLR and mobile photography

13. LITERARY CLUB (Ink & Quill)
    - Focus: Creative writing, poetry, book discussions
    - Activities: Writing competitions, literary magazines, quizzes

=======================================================================
ANNUAL EVENTS
=======================================================================

1. ICONCLAVE — Annual Technical & Cultural Festival
   - Duration: 2–3 days
   - Events include:
     * Hackathon (24-hour coding marathon)
     * Robotics competition
     * CTF (Capture The Flag) cybersecurity contest
     * Coding competition (individual + team)
     * Cultural performances (music, dance, drama)
     * Guest lectures by industry leaders
     * Gaming tournaments
     * Photography contest
     * Debate and quiz competitions
   - Participation: Students from colleges across Maharashtra and beyond

2. FRESHERS' WELCOME (Orientation)
   - Held in August/September for new B.Tech and M.Tech students
   - Introduction to clubs, faculty, and senior students
   - Cultural performances and icebreakers

3. FAREWELL (Seniors' Send-off)
   - Organized by 3rd year students for departing 4th year students
   - Cultural evening and awards

4. SPORTS MEET
   - Annual inter-batch/department sports competition
   - Events: Cricket, football, basketball, athletics, indoor games

5. TECHNICAL WORKSHOPS
   - Conducted throughout the year by clubs and departments
   - Topics: AI/ML, web dev, embedded systems, competitive programming
   - Guest speakers from IITs, industry, startups

6. PLACEMENT TRAINING WORKSHOPS
   - Aptitude training sessions
   - Mock interviews
   - Resume building workshops
   - Coding bootcamps (6–8 weeks before placement season)

=======================================================================
STUDENT GOVERNANCE
=======================================================================

Student Council:
- Elected body representing students across all years
- Acts as bridge between student community and administration
- Roles: President, Vice-President, Secretary, Cultural Secretary, Sports Secretary, Technical Secretary

Hostel Committee:
- Manages hostel-related issues, mess quality feedback

Mess Committee:
- Student representatives who oversee mess quality and suggest improvements

Anti-Ragging Committee:
- Student representatives as part of institute anti-ragging policy
- Contact for ragging complaints: antiragging@iiitp.ac.in

=======================================================================
ACHIEVEMENTS & RECOGNITION
=======================================================================

- Students have cleared GATE with top ranks (AIR < 100 in CSE/ECE)
- Students placed at FAANG companies (Amazon, Google, Nvidia)
- GSoC selections (Google Summer of Code)
- IEEE conference paper publications by B.Tech students
- Smart India Hackathon (SIH) — finalists
- Winner in several IIT-organized hackathons and coding competitions

=======================================================================
HOW TO JOIN CLUBS
=======================================================================

- Club membership drives are held at the beginning of each semester
- First-year students are especially encouraged to join in Semester 1
- No prior experience required for most clubs
- Some technical clubs hold a basic test/interview for advanced members
- All activities are voluntary and free of charge
- Club meetings typically held on weekday evenings or weekends
