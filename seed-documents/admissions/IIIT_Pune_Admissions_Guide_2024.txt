ABOUT IIIT PUNE
=======================================================================

Indian Institute of Information Technology Pune (IIIT Pune) is an Institute of National Importance established in 2016 under the Public Private Partnership (PPP) model of the Ministry of Education (MoE), Government of India. IIIT Pune is one of the fastest-growing IIITs in India, committed to imparting high-quality technical education and research.

Location: Nanoli village, Talegaon Dabhade, Pune district, Maharashtra.
Campus is approximately 35-40 km from Pune city center, near the Mumbai-Pune Expressway. Talegaon Railway Station is approximately 5 km from the campus.

Motto: Vidya Vindate Siddhim (विद्या विन्दते सिद्धिम्) — "Through Knowledge comes Achievement"

Website: https://www.iiitp.ac.in
Contact Email: info@iiitp.ac.in

B.TECH ADMISSION PROCESS
=======================================================================

Entrance Examination:
- Admissions are based on a valid JEE Main score.
- IIIT Pune does NOT accept MHT-CET scores for B.Tech admission.
- Only JEE Main + JoSAA counseling is the valid route.

Counseling Authority:
- Joint Seat Allocation Authority (JoSAA)
- Website: https://josaa.nic.in

Steps for Admission:
1. Appear in JEE Main (conducted by NTA).
2. Register on the JoSAA portal.
3. Fill choices (institute + branch preferences).
4. Participate in seat allotment rounds.
5. Accept allotted seat and pay seat acceptance fee.
6. Report to IIIT Pune for document verification and enrollment.

Eligibility Requirements:
- Must have passed Class 12 (or equivalent) with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics.
- Minimum aggregate: 75% in Class 12 (65% for SC/ST/PwD candidates).
- Must have a valid JEE Main score from the qualifying year.

INTERNATIONAL / NRI ADMISSIONS
=======================================================================

IIIT Pune accepts international students, NRIs, and PIOs through the DASA (Direct Admission of Students Abroad) scheme.
- Website: https://dasanit.org
- Requirements: Class 12 with PCM, SAT/equivalent score as applicable.
- Fee structure for DASA candidates differs from domestic students.
