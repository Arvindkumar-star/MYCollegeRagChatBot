INDIAN INSTITUTE OF INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY PUNE (IIIT Pune)
Fee Structure — B.Tech, M.Tech, Ph.D. Programs
Source: Official IIIT Pune website, JoSAA fee disclosure, careers360
Last Updated: 2024-2025

=======================================================================
B.TECH FEE STRUCTURE (Annual)
=======================================================================

The following is the approximate fee structure for B.Tech students admitted through JoSAA. Fees may vary slightly by batch. Refer to the official IIIT Pune fee structure page for your specific batch.

SEMESTER-WISE FEES (Approximate):

Tuition Fee:
- Tuition Fee per Semester: ₹1,00,000 – ₹1,20,000
- Annual Tuition Fee: ₹2,00,000 – ₹2,40,000

Academic Fees (One-Time / Annual):
- Registration Fee: ₹500 (one-time)
- Examination Fee: ₹1,000 per semester
- Library Fee: ₹500 per semester
- Internet & Computer Lab Fee: ₹1,000 per semester
- Student Activity Fee: ₹500 per semester
- Medical Fee: ₹500 per semester
- Alumni Association Fee: ₹1,000 (one-time at admission)

Refundable Deposits (One-Time):
- Caution Deposit (Library): ₹5,000
- Hostel Security Deposit: ₹5,000–₹10,000

APPROXIMATE TOTAL PER SEMESTER (Academic + Fees): ₹1,10,000 – ₹1,30,000
APPROXIMATE ANNUAL COST (Academic only): ₹2,20,000 – ₹2,60,000

TOTAL COURSE COST (4 Years, Academic Fees Only): ~₹9–11 Lakhs

=======================================================================
HOSTEL FEE STRUCTURE
=======================================================================

Hostel charges are separate from academic fees and are paid directly to the institute hostel office.

Hostel Charges (Per Semester):
- Room Rent (shared, 2-3 occupants): ₹15,000 – ₹20,000 per semester
- Electricity charges: Charged based on actual usage (approximately ₹1,000–₹3,000 per semester)
- Hostel maintenance fee: ₹1,000 per semester

Mess Charges (Per Month):
- Mess fee is typically paid separately to the mess contractor.
- Approximate mess charges: ₹2,500 – ₹3,500 per month
- Annual mess cost (approx.): ₹25,000 – ₹35,000

Annual Hostel + Mess (Approximate): ₹60,000 – ₹80,000

=======================================================================
TOTAL APPROXIMATE ANNUAL COST OF ATTENDANCE
=======================================================================

| Expense              | Amount (Per Year)    |
|----------------------|----------------------|
| Tuition Fee          | ₹2,00,000 – ₹2,40,000 |
| Other Academic Fees  | ₹15,000 – ₹20,000    |
| Hostel (Room+Elec)   | ₹35,000 – ₹50,000    |
| Mess (Food)          | ₹25,000 – ₹35,000    |
| Personal Expenses    | ₹10,000 – ₹20,000    |
| **TOTAL**            | **~₹2,85,000 – ₹3,65,000** |

Total 4-Year Estimated Cost: ₹11–15 Lakhs (including hostel and mess)

=======================================================================
M.TECH FEE STRUCTURE
=======================================================================

M.Tech program duration: 2 years (4 semesters)

Tuition Fee per Semester: ₹50,000 – ₹75,000
Annual Tuition Fee: ₹1,00,000 – ₹1,50,000

GATE Stipend (for CCMT-admitted students with GATE score):
- Monthly stipend: ₹12,400 per month
- This stipend effectively offsets a significant portion of tuition and living costs.

Total 2-Year M.Tech Cost (without stipend): ~₹3–4 Lakhs

=======================================================================
Ph.D. FEE STRUCTURE
=======================================================================

Ph.D. students are typically supported through fellowships:

Fellowship Schemes Available:
1. Institute Fellowship: Monthly stipend as per government norms (~₹31,000 for JRF, ₹35,000 for SRF)
2. Visvesvaraya Ph.D. Scheme (MeitY): Additional support for Ph.D. scholars in electronics and IT
3. Sponsored Category: Fees paid by sponsoring organization

Tuition Fee: Minimal (often waived for fellowship-supported students)

=======================================================================
FEE PAYMENT
=======================================================================

- Fees are paid semester-wise through the institute's online payment portal.
- Payment modes accepted: Net Banking, NEFT/RTGS, Debit/Credit Card.
- Deadline: Usually 2 weeks before the start of each semester.
- Late payment fee: May be applicable for delayed payments.

=======================================================================
FEE CONCESSIONS / EXEMPTIONS
=======================================================================

SC/ST Students:
- Eligible for fee reimbursement under government schemes.
- Apply through the National Scholarship Portal (scholarships.gov.in).

PwD Students:
- May receive fee waivers as per government policy.

EWS Students:
- Eligible for fee waiver under EWS reservation benefits in some cases.
