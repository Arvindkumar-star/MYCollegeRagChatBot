INDIAN INSTITUTE OF INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY PUNE (IIIT Pune)
Scholarships & Financial Aid — Complete Guide
Source: Official IIIT Pune website, NSP portal, careers360, shiksha
Last Updated: 2024

=======================================================================
OVERVIEW
=======================================================================

IIIT Pune does not offer its own institute-funded merit scholarships. However, students are eligible for a wide range of government scholarship schemes at the central and state levels. The institute facilitates students in accessing these schemes.

=======================================================================
CENTRAL GOVERNMENT SCHOLARSHIPS
=======================================================================

1. TOP CLASS EDUCATION SCHOLARSHIP FOR SC STUDENTS
   - Offered by: Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment
   - Eligibility: SC students admitted to top institutions (IIIT Pune qualifies)
   - Benefits: Full tuition fee + maintenance allowance (₹2,220 – ₹3,840/month)
   - Application: National Scholarship Portal (NSP) — scholarships.gov.in
   - Income Limit: Family income < ₹6 lakh per annum
   
2. POST-MATRIC SCHOLARSHIP FOR SC STUDENTS
   - Offered by: State Government (Maharashtra Social Justice Department)
   - Benefits: Tuition fee reimbursement + maintenance allowance
   - Application: State scholarship portal / NSP
   - Income Limit: Generally < ₹2.5 lakh per annum

3. POST-MATRIC SCHOLARSHIP FOR ST STUDENTS
   - Offered by: Tribal Affairs Ministry + State Government
   - Benefits: Tuition fee + hostel charges reimbursement
   - Application: NSP portal / Tribal Affairs portal

4. POST-MATRIC SCHOLARSHIP FOR OBC STUDENTS
   - Offered by: Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment
   - Benefits: Partial fee reimbursement (varies by state)
   - Maharashtra: Administered by Social Welfare Department
   - Income Limit: < ₹1 lakh per annum (central), varies by state

5. MERIT CUM MEANS SCHOLARSHIP (Minority Communities)
   - Offered by: Ministry of Minority Affairs
   - Eligibility: Students from Muslim, Christian, Sikh, Buddhist, Zoroastrian communities with merit + need
   - Benefits: Course fee (up to ₹20,000/year) + maintenance (₹1,000/month)
   - Income Limit: < ₹2.5 lakh per annum
   - Application: NSP portal

6. NATIONAL MEANS CUM MERIT SCHOLARSHIP (NMMSS)
   - Pre-matric scheme (for school level), not directly applicable in college

=======================================================================
STATE GOVERNMENT SCHOLARSHIPS (MAHARASHTRA)
=======================================================================

1. RAJARSHI CHHATRAPATI SHAHU MAHARAJ MERIT SCHOLARSHIP
   - For OBC/SBC/VJNT students from Maharashtra
   - Benefits: Fee reimbursement + maintenance

2. DR. PANJABRAO DESHMUKH VASATIGRUH NIRVAH BHATTA YOJANA
   - Hostel maintenance scholarship for SC/ST students from Maharashtra
   - Benefits: Monthly maintenance allowance

3. SWADHAR GREH SCHEME
   - For SC/Nav-Buddha students who cannot access government hostels
   - Benefits: ₹15,000–₹20,000 per year for food, accommodation, etc.

4. OPEN CATEGORY / EWS SCHOLARSHIP
   - Maharashtra students from EWS with family income < ₹8 lakh
   - Check Maharashtra Scholarship Portal for latest schemes

Application Portal: mahadbocw.in or scholarships.gov.in

=======================================================================
M.TECH GATE STIPEND
=======================================================================

This is NOT a scholarship but a fellowship for M.Tech students:

- Amount: ₹12,400 per month
- Duration: 2 years (entire M.Tech course)
- Eligibility: M.Tech students admitted through CCMT with a valid GATE score
- Funded by: AICTE / Ministry of Education
- This stipend effectively offsets most living expenses during M.Tech.

=======================================================================
Ph.D. FELLOWSHIPS
=======================================================================

1. Institute Fellowship (IIIT Pune):
   - Junior Research Fellow (JRF): ₹31,000/month for first 2 years
   - Senior Research Fellow (SRF): ₹35,000/month from 3rd year onwards
   - Duration: Up to 5 years (subject to academic progress review)
   - Contingency Grant: ₹20,000/year for research expenses

2. Visvesvaraya Ph.D. Scheme (MeitY):
   - Extra ₹7,500/month top-up over institute fellowship
   - For Ph.D. scholars in Electronics, IT, and related fields
   - Funded by Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology

3. External Fellowships (applicable for IIIT Pune scholars):
   - Prime Minister Research Fellowship (PMRF): ₹70,000–₹80,000/month (very competitive)
   - CSIR-UGC JRF: For NET-qualified students
   - DST Inspire Fellowship: For top-ranked B.Tech/M.Tech graduates

=======================================================================
EDUCATION LOANS
=======================================================================

Banks offering education loans for IIIT Pune students:

1. SBI Scholar Loan:
   - For premier institutions (IIIT Pune qualifies)
   - Amount: Up to ₹30–40 lakhs (no collateral for amounts up to ₹7.5L)
   - Interest rate: 8.5–10% per annum
   - Moratorium: Course duration + 1 year

2. Canara Bank Vidya Turant:
   - Similar terms for premier institutions

3. Bank of Baroda Baroda Vidya Loan:
   - Available for IIITs

4. HDFC Credila Education Loan:
   - Private lender with fast processing

5. Axis Bank Education Loan:
   - Up to ₹20 lakhs without collateral

Required Documents for Education Loan:
- Admission letter from IIIT Pune (JoSAA allotment + institute confirmation)
- Fee structure document
- Parent/guardian income proof (last 3 years ITR)
- Bank statements (6 months)
- Passport-size photos
- Aadhaar + PAN (student + co-borrower)
- Property documents (if collateral required for high-value loans)

HOW TO APPLY — SCHOLARSHIP PROCESS
=======================================================================

Step 1: Register on National Scholarship Portal (NSP)
Website: https://scholarships.gov.in
- Create account using Aadhaar / mobile number

Step 2: Complete your profile
- Fill in personal, academic, and bank details accurately

Step 3: Search for applicable schemes
- Filter by category (SC/ST/OBC/Minority/EWS), state, course level

Step 4: Upload required documents
Common documents needed:
  - Category certificate (Caste Certificate)
  - Caste Validity Certificate (Maharashtra)
  - Income Certificate (from Tehsildar/SDM) — for current financial year
  - Domicile Certificate
  - Aadhaar Card
  - JoSAA Allotment Letter + IIIT Pune Admission Confirmation
  - Previous academic marksheets (10th, 12th)
  - Bank account details (student's own account, preferably nationalized bank)
  - Passport-size photograph

Step 5: Submit application and note the reference number

Step 6: Institute verification
- The IIIT Pune scholarship office / Students' Section verifies applications
- Ensure you inform the Students' Section about your application

Step 7: Disbursement
- Scholarship amount is credited directly to student's bank account (DBT)
