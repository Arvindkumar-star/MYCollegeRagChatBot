INDIAN INSTITUTE OF INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY PUNE (IIIT Pune)
Hostel, Campus Life & Facilities Guide
Source: Official website, collegedunia, careers360, student reviews
Last Updated: 2024

=======================================================================
CAMPUS OVERVIEW
=======================================================================

Location: Nanoli village, Talegaon Dabhade, Pune - 410506, Maharashtra
Setting: Scenic campus near the Sahyadri mountain ranges
Distance from Pune city: ~35-40 km
Distance from Talegaon Railway Station: ~5 km
Distance from Mumbai: ~100 km via Mumbai-Pune Expressway

The campus is set in a serene, green environment away from the city noise, ideal for focused academic life. The area is known for its pleasant climate throughout the year.

=======================================================================
HOSTEL FACILITIES
=======================================================================

Availability:
- On-campus hostel accommodation is provided for BOTH boys and girls.
- Allotment is based on availability; most students get accommodation.
- Separate hostel blocks for boys and girls.

Room Types:
- Shared rooms (typically 2–3 occupants per room)
- Triple occupancy is common in most blocks
- Some double-occupancy rooms may be available for senior students

Room Amenities:
- Single bed per student
- Study table and chair
- Almirah / wardrobe for storage
- Ceiling fan
- Power outlets for laptop/device charging
- Shared bathrooms (one per few rooms or per floor)
- Common washing machines available (coin-operated or free)

Hostel Facilities:
- 24x7 security at hostel gates
- Biometric/ID card-based entry
- CCTV surveillance
- Common rooms / recreation areas
- Reading rooms / study halls
- Indoor games (carrom, chess, table tennis)
- Wi-Fi connectivity (available in academic areas; hostel Wi-Fi is improving)

=======================================================================
MESS AND FOOD
=======================================================================

Mess System:
- The campus has a centralized mess facility
- Separate messes or common mess for boys and girls (varies by year)
- Operated by a contracted mess vendor / student-managed committee

Meal Schedule (Typical):
- Breakfast: 7:30 AM – 9:00 AM
- Lunch: 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM
- Snacks / Evening Tea: 4:30 PM – 5:30 PM
- Dinner: 7:30 PM – 9:30 PM

Food Quality:
- Primarily vegetarian meals
- Special non-vegetarian meals on specific days (Sunday/Thursday in some messes)
- Student mess committee provides feedback mechanism to improve quality
- Festive special meals during festivals (Diwali, Holi, etc.)

Mess Fee: ₹2,500 – ₹3,500 per month (paid separately to mess contractor)

Additional Food Options:
- Canteen / Snack shop on campus
- Talegaon town is accessible for outside food options (auto-rickshaw/bike)

=======================================================================
INTERNET AND CONNECTIVITY
=======================================================================

Campus Network:
- High-speed internet in academic buildings (server rooms, labs, library)
- Wi-Fi available across campus
- Wired internet connections in some hostel rooms
- Speed: Generally 100 Mbps+ shared bandwidth
- Students are assigned individual login credentials for network access

Mobile Network:
- Airtel, Jio, BSNL coverage available on campus
- Signal strength may vary in some hostel buildings (use outdoor for better signal)

=======================================================================
MEDICAL FACILITIES
=======================================================================

- Dispensary on campus with a resident doctor
- First aid available 24/7
- Tie-up with nearby hospitals for emergency cases
- Ambulance available during emergencies
- Students are encouraged to have personal health insurance

Nearest Hospital:
- Talegaon Dabhade Urban Primary Health Center (~5 km)
- Pune city hospitals accessible via expressway in ~40 minutes

=======================================================================
LIBRARY
=======================================================================

- Central Library with physical books and reference materials
- Digital library access (IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library, Springer, Elsevier)
- Reading rooms open till late evening / 24 hours during exams
- Photocopying and printing facility

=======================================================================
TRANSPORT AND CONNECTIVITY
=======================================================================

- MSRTC (State Transport) buses to Talegaon and Pune
- Talegaon Railway Station: ~5 km (local trains to Pune and Mumbai)
- Auto-rickshaws and shared cabs available near the institute gate
- Some students maintain personal bikes/cycles

Institute Bus:
- Limited shuttle service for students going to Talegaon for essential needs
- Check current schedule with the hostel office

=======================================================================
HOSTEL RULES (GENERAL)
=======================================================================

- Guests/outsiders not allowed in hostel rooms without prior permission
- Night curfew: Usually 11 PM for boys, earlier for girls (check current policy)
- Ragging is strictly prohibited under UGC anti-ragging regulations
- Possession of alcohol, drugs strictly banned
- Noise restrictions after 10 PM in hostel blocks

=======================================================================
HOSTEL APPLICATION
=======================================================================

- Hostel is allotted to students at the time of admission
- Application is part of the admission process through the institute portal
- Priority is given to outstation students and first-year students
- Students wishing to opt out of hostel must formally notify the hostel office
